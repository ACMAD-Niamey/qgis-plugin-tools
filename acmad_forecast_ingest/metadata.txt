name=ACMAD Forecast Ingest
qgisMinimumVersion=3.16
description=Upload drought forecast polygon shapefiles directly from QGIS to the ACMAD Drought Advisory backend (Django REST API), replacing manual upload processes.
about=Lets a domain expert pick a seasonal drought forecast shapefile (.shp/.shx/.dbf/.prj) from disk, fill in the forecast metadata (period, lead name, date produced, year), and upload it in one click to the ACMAD Drought Advisory backend's forecast ingestion endpoint. The shapefile parts are zipped client-side and posted as multipart/form-data over Qt's own network stack, authenticated with a pre-issued DRF API token stored in QGIS settings. No self-service login: tokens are obtained out-of-band from a backend administrator.
